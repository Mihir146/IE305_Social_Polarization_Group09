4 / 16 · Agent rules

A conversation changes beliefs. A purchase needs value AND budget.

1 INFORM

An aware contact shares launch news with probability 0.25.

2 DISCUSS

Close beliefs compromise: 0.40 and 0.50 become 0.43 and 0.47.

3 BUY ONCE

Aware + can afford + perceived value ≥ price. Purchases at period end.

4 EXPERIENCE

New owners receive personal feedback. Beliefs and later conversations can change.

Rules: MODEL_SPEC.md. d=0.2; mu=0.3; phi=0.05. Worked opinion example is illustrative.

15 / 16 · Backup · Limitations

What we did not establish.

• No real demand calibration or empirical sales validation.
• No global optimum: only three prices and two sample quantities.
• No costly network-data collection, competition, inventory or fixed overhead.
• Hard confidence, independent attributes and informed-nonbuyer sharing are assumptions.
• Finite campaign outcomes are not equilibrium claims.
• Pointwise intervals do not protect every comparison simultaneously.
• Actual engine evidence must be kept separate from reference and Java runs.
