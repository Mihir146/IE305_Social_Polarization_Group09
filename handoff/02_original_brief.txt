## Project idea: When Social Choice Creates Social Polarization

We want to investigate whether a population that begins with a socially mixed network can spontaneously develop polarized opinions and disconnected communities through individual decisions about whom to interact with.

Each agent represents a person with an opinion, tolerance for disagreement, and susceptibility to social influence. Agents interact through a social network. Interactions can change opinions, while disagreement can probabilistically weaken or break connections. Agents may then form new connections with people whose opinions are more similar. This creates feedback between opinion dynamics and network structure, potentially producing polarization and network fragmentation.

**Central research question:** Under what conditions do individual-level opinion updates and adaptive social connections cause a connected, mixed population to become polarized or fragmented?
